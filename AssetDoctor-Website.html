Founder story

Meet Ashutosh — The 14-Year-Old Mastermind Behind Asset Doctor

Like millions of Indian families, Ashutosh saw the chaos of lost warranty bills, expired RCs, forgotten PUC dates, and sudden roadside emergencies with no documents ready.

So he built Asset Doctor — a smart utility vault that helps everyday people safely store vehicle papers, track home appliance warranties, and get timely renewal alerts — before problems become penalties.
