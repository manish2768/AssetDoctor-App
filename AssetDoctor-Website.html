Security & privacy

Your documents. Your device. Your rules.

Asset Doctor is built local-first — your data works for you, never for advertisers.

256-bit Encryption

Every document and record is encrypted in transit and at rest inside Firebase, isolated per user account.

Local-First Privacy

Data is cached on-device first and synced only to your own vault — never pooled, profiled, or resold.

Zero Unsolicited Ads

No third-party ad networks, no spam notifications — only the reminders you set and the partners you tap.

Founder story

Meet Ashutosh — The 14-Year-Old Prodigy Behind Asset Doctor

Like millions of Indian families, Ashutosh saw the chaos of lost warranty bills, expired RCs, forgotten PUC dates, and sudden roadside emergencies with no documents ready.

At just 14, he designed and built Asset Doctor from the ground up — a smart utility vault that helps everyday people safely store vehicle papers, track home appliance warranties, connect instantly with service partners, and get timely renewal alerts before problems become penalties.
