Privacy Policy

Asset Doctor (package com.assetdoctor.app) · Last updated August 2026

1. Data we collect

Document images/PDFs you upload, offline cache metadata, and notification tokens. App Lock uses your phone PIN, pattern, or biometrics — Asset Doctor never stores a separate app password.

2. How we use data

To provide Asset Doctor features: vault storage, expiry alerts, health/resale estimates, power cost tracking, WhatsApp OTP login / reminders you opt into, and document sharing you initiate.

3. Storage & security

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage) under your account. Access is protected by authentication rules owned by your user ID.

4. Permissions

Camera/photos for invoices and asset documents; file access for PDFs; notifications for PUC, insurance and warranty reminders. Asset Doctor never reads your SMS inbox.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Documents are shared only when you tap WhatsApp/share. Google Sign-In uses Google OAuth. WhatsApp Cloud API is used only for authentication OTP, welcome messages, and service reminders you have not opted out of.

6. Your controls

You may edit profile data, delete assets/documents, turn off WhatsApp reminders in Settings, or sign out. Contact the developer to request account deletion.
